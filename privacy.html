Inkly privacy

Inkly is a classroom tool made by our district's technology team. It is used by staff on school computers with the Inkly app for Mac and the Inkly Chrome extension.

What Inkly keeps

The ink you draw on web pages, with the address and title of each page so the ink appears there again; your Inkly Documents (whiteboard pages, imported PDFs, Google Slides and Docs, SMART Notebook files) and autosaved copies of them; and your settings. All of this is stored in Chrome and the Inkly app on the computer you use. Inkly uses your pen, finger and on-screen keyboard input only to draw and type on the screen; it does not record it.

Google Drive

When you choose to open a file from, or save a document to, Google Drive, Inkly signs in to your own Google account (through Chrome) and reads or writes only the files you pick or save. It shows your account's name and email so you can see which account is connected. The files stay in your Drive, and the sign-in stays in Chrome.

What Inkly does not do

Inkly does not send your ink, documents, browsing or any other information to its developer or to anyone else. It has no advertising or analytics, and nothing is sold or shared.

Removing your data

Clear ink from a page or delete documents in Inkly at any time. Removing the extension deletes what it stored in Chrome. Files you saved to Google Drive remain yours.
